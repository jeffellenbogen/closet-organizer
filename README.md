# Closet

A quick, clickable mockup of a phone app for organizing your closet.

**Try it on your phone:** https://jeffellenbogen.github.io/closet-organizer/
On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen to open it full screen like an app.

## What's in the mockup

- **Closet**: every piece hangs on a rail by category (shoes and accessories sit on a shelf), sorted light to dark by color. Filter by season or search by name or color.
- **Item details**: tap a piece to see how often you wear it, when you last wore it, and mark it worn today.
- **Add a piece**: name, category, type, color and seasons, with a live preview.
- **Outfits**: shuffle an outfit from what's in season right now and save the ones you like.
- **Unworn**: anything you haven't worn in 3 months, with Keep or Donate.

Everything is saved in your browser only. "Reset demo" at the bottom of the Closet screen brings back the sample closet.

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` in any browser. There's no build step and nothing to install.
